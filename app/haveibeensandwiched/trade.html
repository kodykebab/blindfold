PREVIEW / RUNS ON YOUR MACHINE

Trading runs locally, by design

Your key, your sealed orders and your zero-knowledge proofs never touch a server, so this hosted page is a read-only preview. To trade, run Blindfold on your machine and open the page it prints:

git clone https://github.com/kodykebab/blindfold && cd blindfold
source env.sh
WEB_PORT=4400 scripts/dev.sh      # then open http://127.0.0.1:4400/trade

Setup steps: Getting started.

Export a backup now. Without it, your order can’t be revealed or recovered if this machine is lost. Export a private backup, then confirm.

          YOUR PRIVATE STATE

          Back up & restore

          After committing an order, export a backup and keep it somewhere private. Your order, salt and funding state are needed for recovery.

          The exported file is not encrypted. It holds your orders and salts: store it like a password. Backups contain private data and are not encrypted by the CLI. Restoring checks the account, deployment and onchain commitments.
